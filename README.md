# 🧞 GigGenie

### Your skills. Your opportunities. Your next move.

GigGenie is an AI-powered opportunity agent for students.

Instead of simply showing a list of internships and hackathons, GigGenie understands a student's **skills, interests and goals**, searches opportunities, checks eligibility, explains why an opportunity matches, identifies skill gaps, and creates a personalized action plan.

## 🚀 Problem

Students find internships, hackathons and competitions across many platforms, but it is difficult to know:

- Which opportunities actually match their profile?
- Am I eligible?
- Why is this opportunity relevant to me?
- What skills am I missing?
- What should I do next?

## 💡 Solution

GigGenie follows an agentic workflow:

**Understand → Search → Check Eligibility → Match → Reason → Plan → Deliver**

The agent provides:

- 🎯 Personalized opportunity matching
- ✅ Eligibility checking
- 🧠 Explainable reasoning
- 📚 Skill-gap detection
- 🗺️ Personalized next-action plans
- 🔗 Direct opportunity links
- 🌐 Live opportunity data when available
